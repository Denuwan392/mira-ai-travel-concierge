## 4. Rate Workbook (`rate_sheet_2026-27.xlsx`)

The canonical Excel rate workbook contains 6 worksheets:
1. `READ ME`: Pricing instructions, calculation formulas, and currency conventions (LKR to USD via fixed exchange rate).
2. `Settings`: Global exchange rates, tax percentages (VAT / SSCL), and profit margins.
3. `Seasons`: Date ranges for Peak Season, High Season, and Standard Season across 2026–2027.
4. `Tours`: Per-person package rates for standard tour packages across seasons and passenger group tiers.
5. `Hotels`: Room rates, meal supplement plans (BB, HB, FB), child extra bed charges, and seasonal hotel surcharges.
6. `Transport`: Per-day vehicle hire rates, fuel surcharges, driver-guide accommodation allowances, and highway toll inclusions.
