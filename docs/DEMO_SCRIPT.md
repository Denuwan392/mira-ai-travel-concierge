- **0:40–1:45** Send “Sigiriya day trip for two adults on 10 January 2027.” Open the source, show USD 315 with full deposit, and choose the mock declined simulation before retrying successfully.

- **2:30–3:35** Open the staff console with Editor credentials. Generate an Ella draft and preview its provenance. Sign in as Approver, approve explicitly, and ask Mira for the published title. Explain local extractive mode when Gemini is absent.

## 30-minute live demo and questions

Use three minutes on architecture, twelve on quick booker, planner, mock failure and returning visitor, six on staff governance and trace, then five on the report and a calculation change. Use the remaining time for team inputs and the 15-minute question period that follows.

For a controlled pricing change, copy the full dataset into a scratch folder outside the employer delivery. Modify Settings only in the scratch copy and run with `MIRA_DATA` pointing there. Show the new quote, the changed workbook digest and the expected failure of old fixed goldens. Point back to the original source and rerun before closing. Keep credentials, filenames and real traveller information out of the recording.
