Business Problem & Implemented Solution

Ceylon Compass Holidays required a conversational booking assistant serving two distinct personas: quick bookers seeking instant, exact quotes for published tours, and custom journey planners needing multi-day itineraries without fabricated prices.

  • Canonical Workspace: React + Tailwind v4 SPA (/workspace/index.html) paired with a unified Staff & Admin Console (/staff).
  • 5-Node LangGraph Orchestration: Explicit interpret → retrieve → quote / compose → save pipeline over FastAPI and @ag-ui/client SSE.
  • Zero LLM Arithmetic: Server-side Python Decimal engine (mira.data.price) calculates quotes from an unchanged SHA-256 workbook snapshot.
  • Human-Governed Publishing: Drafts stay private until an authenticated approver reviews and publishes them.
Core Architectural Decisions & Methodology
  • Local-Rule vs. Gemini Separation: Regex entity extraction and tag retrieval provide deterministic offline execution; Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) is invoked selectively for custom itinerary parsing, suggestion ranking, and AI drafting when configured.
  • Strict A2UI Catalog Validation: All 17 component types inherit from Pydantic Strict(extra="forbid") and mirror Zod schemas on the client.
  • Automated + Qualitative Eval: 54 AG-UI conversation cases (17 supplied + 37 authored, 7 multi-turn) executed in local deterministic mode on 2026-11-24, plus 10 LLM judge cases scored on 2026-11-23.
  • Regression Recovery (2026-11-25): Live browser recording exposed 3 Scene 02 workspace defects; all 3 were fixed and verified by 7 targeted regression tests plus 220 pytest and 70 Vitest checks.
Metric Boundary & Integrity Note

Do not conflate overlapping metrics: Overall case success (53/54, 98.1%) measures all 9 requirement categories, whereas deterministic pricing accuracy (23/24, 95.8%) measures only the 24 exact-quote cases—where the sole discrepancy (P03) is the documented Q-1043 historical email omission of child extra beds. Similarly, 10/10 LLM judge scores reflect automated rubric verdicts; independent human calibration remains pending (human_scores: null).

Remaining Operational Limitations (Disclosed)
  • Human Calibration Pending: 10 review cases scored by gemini-3.5-flash-lite await independent human labels in evals/judges/human_labels.json.
  • External Tracing Verification: SQLite local tracing is verified end-to-end; hosted Langfuse cloud delivery was not verified in offline/demo runs.
  • Full Eval Post-Fix: After Scene 02 fixes (2026-11-25), 7 targeted regression tests, make test, and 6/6 demo recordings passed; the full 54-case runner was not re-executed.
  • Single-Host SQLite & Mock Checkout: Designed for local evaluation; payment simulation is mock-only and horizontal scaling requires PostgreSQL.

Scene 02 Failure Diagnosis, Fixes & Regression Suite

During end-to-end Playwright recording of the custom-to-package journey (Scene 02), automated checks caught three real integration bugs despite passing backend unit tests. All three were diagnosed from trace/DOM artifacts and fixed with targeted regression coverage.

BEFORE (02-failure.png · Trace 7369e3be…): Backend returned valid T09 quote for 2027-05-03 (USD 2,125), but workspace picker retained 2027-01-10, flagging Quote Outdated and disabling mock checkout.
AFTER (02-verified.png · Trace c9ae1e28…): Picker, date (2027-05-03), and party (2 adults) synchronize on quote receipt/restore; mock checkout enabled while genuine post-quote edits still invalidate checkout.
AFTER (02-journey.png): 10-day itinerary with Ella & Kandy photos, interactive RouteMap pins, and 3 published TourCard recommendations.
Three Root Causes Identified & Fixed
  • 1. Stale Frontend Travel Date (02-diagnostic.json): Workspace.jsx initialized 2027-01-10 and applied PriceBreakdown without hydrating picker state. Fix: Synchronize picker date/party/ages upon quote receipt or trip restore while preserving stale-quote checkout lock when the user subsequently edits inputs.
  • 2. Map Not Dispatched to Renderer: InteractiveMap passed server validation and SSE transport but had no active dispatch branch. Fix: Wired existing RouteMap with published destination coordinates; clicking pins switches day & photo.
  • 3. Missing Photos & Suppressed Cards: Plan renderer lacked destination photo lookup and suppressed retrieved TourCards. Fix: Connected shared photo registry and published destination tour recommendations.
7 Targeted Regression Tests Added & Verified (2026-11-25)

4 Focused API Tests (test_family_planning_workflow.py): Verified broad clarification without 2-day default/enquiry, 6-day grounded itinerary without unrelated cards, package switch retiring prior proposal cleanly, and date edits retaining child age until an adult-only package replaces it.

3 Workspace Tests (scene02-workspace.test.tsx): Verified January family config replacement on receipt & restore, checkout lock preservation after manual date edit, and route-pin/photo/recommendation interaction. Note: Full 54-case eval suite was not rerun after this UI/state fix.
